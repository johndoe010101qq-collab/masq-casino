MASQ ROULETTE WHEEL — 2026-10-15

Scope: replace the entire rejected wheel, including the old block-built bowl,
grey separators and floating raster number ring. Keep the uploaded studio and
operator footage, existing release mark, accepted result and financial clock.
Dice, Coinflip, Plinko, Upgrader, Blackjack and slots are outside this redesign.

The replacement is an original procedural Three.js hard-surface model. Its
turned wooden bowl, metal track, deep37 pocket floors, thin separators, printed
number band, rotor cone and weighted spindle are real geometry. Shape/material
design is inferred; it does not claim to copy a supplied wheel design.
The original footprint and the lower base/cross envelopes are preserved to
cover the old baked wheel without reconstructing the filmed dealer's hands.

Authoring master: src/roulette-wheel-model-v48.js, reproducible without an
external asset application. Browser uses CanvasTexture for37 canonical labels,
fixed material grain and instance colors. Headless validation supplies its own
canvas. No model RNG, frame clock or payout code is introduced.

Film-world Y remains up. Center[0,14.5,4.6]; trackr9/floor15.77, pocketr5.25/
floor14.50; ballradius.42. The off-axis projection uses exact supplied eye and
frustum offsets instead of an approximate lookAt. Ball draws at or above the
model surface to prevent penetrating the number band; accepted final position,
source release, round progress and settlement remain unchanged.

ClassicFilmStage sourceSpace mode places footage, poster and complete WebGL
layer in one fixed native1920x1080/720x960 surface. Fit and camera transforms
apply to their shared parent. Existing other film scenes keep the old default.

Validation: test:roulette-wheel, shared media lifecycle checks and existing
roulette accepted-outcome/release checks, then native desktop/mobile QA.
No new media or third-party executable from the uploaded archive was run.
