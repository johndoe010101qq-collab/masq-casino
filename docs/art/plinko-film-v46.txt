CALIBRATION
No machine-readable camera or hand tracks accompanied the files. Source-space
anchors were calibrated from the two layout guides and checked in the browser:
desktop centre960; pin half-step30.9; first ball contactY459; row step48.2;
tray landingY864; multiplier labelY937. The ninth, final segment ends at its
explicit tray anchor rather than applying a single Y scale to the old 3D board.
Mobile source mapping is x'=5/6*x-440, y'=5/6*y+42. Native footage always fits
the stage height. Narrow stages crop spare side walls; short desktop stages
letterbox horizontally instead of cutting off the head or bottom multipliers.
Hand/ball overlay positions are calibrated approximations, not supplied mocap.

CLOCK AND CONCURRENCY
The existing PlinkoFlightController alone accepts and settles rounds. Its
3800ms duration, current path, balance checks and unlimited concurrent launches
are unchanged. Renderer or media failure cannot delay or repeat settlement.
The decorative actor scheduler receives only ids and public progress values.
Rapid inputs share a whole-character gesture; there are at most current/next
gesture groups, never one queued animation per paid round or an admission cap.
All accepted ids retain independent overlay paths. Release is frame17; the
physical inlet hides the ball until the nozzle. Reduced motion/shortened
deadlines use the nozzle fallback. No ball waits in the hand after its round.
The idle restarts from neutral after a gesture to avoid a mid-loop pose cut.
Native idle pauses when hidden, manually paused or reduced motion is enabled.
The render clock is controller-owned; no additional requestAnimationFrame,
timers, result selection, stake writes, RNG or financial calls live here.
